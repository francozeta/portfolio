# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is international clients looking for a product-minded developer and designer who can turn early ideas into clear, polished web interfaces. A secondary audience includes collaborators, potential Kocteau users, and teams in Peru and Latin America evaluating Franco's work, judgment, and availability.

Visitors need to understand what Franco builds, see credible evidence of product and engineering skill, and find a direct path to start a conversation. English is the primary language for now; the system must remain ready for a complete Spanish version rather than mixing languages on the same surface.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio earns client trust, grows Franco Zeta's personal brand, and uses Kocteau as the clearest proof of end-to-end product work. It should connect writing, case studies, code, and product decisions into one coherent picture: Franco can shape an idea, build the interface and underlying system, and care for the details people actually use.

Success means qualified clients and collaborators can quickly understand the offer, remember the work, and contact Franco with confidence. The site should also strengthen Kocteau by giving its product story a credible home within Franco's broader body of work.

## Brand Personality

Sober, precise, and personal. The voice is direct, human, and quietly confident. It should feel like a careful design engineer's working index rather than a sales funnel. Jakub Krehel's portfolio is a reference for its compact reading flow, restraint, and strong connection between personal voice, projects, and writing.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS landing page, an agency template, or an AI-generated portfolio. Avoid gradients, neon accents, decorative glassmorphism, corporate marketing language, aggressive sales sections, oversized claims, excessive animation, and repetitive icon-card grids. Do not mix English and Spanish within the same page unless the bilingual experience is intentionally designed and fully translated.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work, not the claim.** Let Kocteau, case studies, writing, and concrete decisions prove capability.
2. **Quiet confidence.** Make the value clear without inflating it or turning the page into a pitch deck.
3. **One strong reading flow.** Keep the experience compact, legible, and free of competing calls to action.
4. **Personal, not casual.** Write in Franco's voice while keeping client-facing information precise and internationally understandable.
5. **International reach, Lima roots.** Lead in English while preserving the credibility and perspective of building from Peru.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, meaningful accessible names, sufficient contrast, 200% text zoom, and reflow at 320 CSS pixels. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; movement should never be required to understand or operate the site. A future Spanish locale must translate complete pages, metadata, navigation, and structured signals instead of creating partial or mixed-language variants.
